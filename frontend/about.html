FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is TruthCheck free to use?▾
Yes, TruthCheck is completely free to use. Simply visit the Verify page, paste your content, and get an instant credibility score.
How accurate is TruthCheck?▾
TruthCheck achieves 94.2% overall accuracy. English content reaches 97%, while regional languages range from 91–95% accuracy depending on the language.
Can I check WhatsApp forwards?▾
Yes. Simply copy the forwarded message text and paste it into the WhatsApp tab on the Verify page. TruthCheck will analyze it instantly.
Which languages are supported?▾
TruthCheck supports English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, and Bengali. More languages are coming soon.
Is my data stored or shared?▾
No. Your submitted content is sent only to the AI analysis engine and is not stored, logged, or shared with third parties.
